# AI_USAGE.md

The DA351 policy allows AI tools but requires a record of (1) the tool used, (2) the prompts used, and
(3) how the output was modified. **Add a row every time you use an AI tool, and fill in the
"How we modified it" column yourselves.** That column is what shows your own contribution.
